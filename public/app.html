Security at a glance

  • Vault encrypted on-device (AES-256-GCM, non-extractable key).
  • Shared copies: encrypted before upload; key only in the QR fragment.
  • Signed, expiring tokens verified on every request.
  • Revocation destroys the server-side ciphertext.
  • Honest limit: revoking can't un-see what was already displayed.
